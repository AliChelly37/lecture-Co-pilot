Welcome back everyone. Today we continue with phase equilibrium, and in particular the concept of fugacity. Fugacity is the effective pressure that replaces the real pressure in the expression for the chemical potential of a real gas. For an ideal gas the fugacity equals the pressure, and the fugacity coefficient is exactly one. For a real gas we compute the fugacity coefficient from an equation of state, and the one we will use in this course is the Peng-Robinson equation of state.
